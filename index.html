How I work

Deep in one thing, useful across many.

I go deep on growth economics: CAC, LTV, cohorts, payback. Around that core I work across product, positioning, lifecycle, content, PR, and operations. T-shaped on purpose.

Most growth problems aren't marketing problems.

They're positioning, product friction, or incentive problems. My job is to find the real constraint before spending budget on the wrong one.

Goals first, tactics second.

My favorite question is "why?" If the goal, the success metric, and the outcome aren't clear, execution is just noise.

I do a lot with a lean team.

I've built functions from scratch and run them without big headcount. I own outcomes, not just outputs, and I move across whatever needs to move.

AI as leverage, not decoration.

AI removes the grunt work so I spend my time on judgment and prioritization. It's why one experienced person can now cover ground that used to need a team. This site is one small example: I built it myself with AI.
